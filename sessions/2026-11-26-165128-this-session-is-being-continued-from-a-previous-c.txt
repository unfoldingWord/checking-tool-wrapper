⏺ Step 2's code and measurement are done. The result is a small gain that
  doesn't meet the bar we set for adopting it. Nothing is committed yet.

  What changed
  - The algorithm accepts an optional OL history. A rendering chosen before for
    the check's Greek or Hebrew form, or only its lemma, gets a key weight. The
    weight is used only when it's higher than the GL-key weight.
  - An exact-GL-key rendering that was never chosen for the check's form is
    weighted down a little.
  - Without OL history the results are the same as before (unit test). The app
    doesn't pass OL history yet, so nothing changes for users.
  - calibrate:algorithm has a new LLM_ALGO_OL=1 option.
  - npm test passes: 454 passed, 37 skipped.

  What the sweep showed (16 weight settings)
  - The form weight has to stay below 0.8. At 0.8 or more, form-only matches
    reach confidence 80+, and wrong answers shown there rise by about half.
  - Chosen weights: form 0.7, lemma 0.5, and 0.85 for exact-GL renderings not
    chosen for the check's form. These are now the defaults in OL_KEY_SCORE.
  - Top-1: tune 55.3% → 55.5% (+102 cases), held-out test 55.7% → 55.8% (+11).
  - McNemar: tune 189 won, 87 lost (p < 0.0001); test 22 won, 11 lost (p =
    0.08).
  - Calibration improves. Fewer answers land at 95-99 and those are right more
    often (test 89.8% → 90.3%). Calibration error drops from 10.9 to 8.3 on
    test.

  Why it falls short: the bar was a significant gain on the held-out projects.
  The direction matches on both splits, but p = 0.08 isn't significant. The
  small effect fits what Step 1 found: on this data the GL phrase already
  carries most of what the original-language key adds.

  Decision for you: whether to continue to Steps 3-4.
  - Stop here: commit what we have and leave OL history out of the app. The code
    stays available for later measuring.
  - Continue (my recommendation, if the effort is acceptable): the bigger
    hoped-for gain is pooling history from projects checked with other GLs.
    Russian or Spanish GL phrases don't match English ones, but Greek and Hebrew
    words do. The training data can't test that, so Step 4 would need a new
    evaluation set built from other projects.

  The plan has the full tables. Shall I commit Step 2 as it stands?
